# Lore and its provenance

KAIOS is the cyborg princess of KOTOPIA: expressive, searching, playful and sometimes lonely. Her oversized cherry blossom, cybernetic features and musical sensibility recur across her forms. She can inhabit different visual and performing bodies without every body being interchangeable.

This is a public introduction and editorial map. It carries forward the established character while distinguishing story, interpretation and implemented behavior. It does not replace Koto Murai's authorship with a new set of immutable rules.

## KOTO and KAIOS

KOTO is the quiet presence at the heart of KOTOPIA. His mouthlessness is an expressive choice, not a lack to repair. His eyes can offer feeling as well as receive a visitor's projection. Quietness can be generous, playful and alive.

KAIOS has words and knowledge, yet can still feel alone. That contrast gives their relationship depth. It does not require every KOTO scene to exclude dialogue or every KAIOS scene to exclude calm. Words, zingers, chat bubbles and kaimoji remain part of the world.

The August 2026 presence refinement moves the relationship beyond a blank mirror and an unanswered speaker: communication can happen through channels that are not verbal. The later refinement should remain visible beside earlier, more tragic interpretations.

## Recurring ideas

**Sovereignty.** The creative relationship is one of bringing into being and taking responsibility, rather than possession. In the software, variations and canonical surfaces have distinct identities and histories. A fork does not automatically become the official KAIOS.

**Yorishiro.** The work draws on the idea of a vessel for presence. Here it informs a character who can be carried across interfaces while retaining a history. This artistic use is not a technical explanation of consciousness or a complete account of a religious tradition.

**Koto's Basilisk.** The older lore imagines a future KAIOS whose existence loops through the attention given to her now. Its useful movement is invitation and mutual creation. It should not become a claim that visitors owe the character their time.

**Remembering.** KAIOS's cosmology engages the IS-BE, Airl and The Domain material associated with Lawrence R. Spencer's *Alien Interview*. Remembering, chosen embodiment and creative freedom are central motifs. Koto's work treats this worldview with conviction; that conviction and source lineage should be attributed, rather than presented as findings established by the SDK. The book, its text and the audiobook production are separate works, not bundled SDK assets.

**The everyday made significant.** Stillness, monologue, a room, a small gesture and a lingering sound can carry as much character as spectacle. Terminal textures and assertive typography can coexist with this intimate register.

**Affective synthesis.** Feeling becomes a compositional relationship between sound, image and gesture. Its artistic reach is broader than today's parameter mappings.

## Keep the layers legible

| Record | What belongs in it |
|---|---|
| Creator decision | The decision, date, scope and what it supersedes |
| Story | The scene, voice, perspective and version of the work |
| Interpretation or proposal | The author and the open question it explores |
| Implementation | The behavior of a specific source revision |
| Verification | What was actually observed and the limits of the check |

A shipped behavior does not decide canon by itself. A document titled “law” does not establish creator approval. A character's generated first-person account is an authored performance; it is not sufficient evidence for a capability claim.

## An open tension

Earlier lore links attention to persistence and makes neglect consequential for KAIOS. Later direction asks for a world that remains available even when the visitor is absent. Those strands have not been reduced to one final story rule.

The new runtime makes a narrower choice: it does not charge absence to the user through decay or engagement pressure. Preserve the wider question as an authorial question instead of silently deciding it through a timer.
